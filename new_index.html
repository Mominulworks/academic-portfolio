About

I'm Mominul Islam, an undergraduate student currently in my third semester of a Computer Science & Engineering degree. My first two semesters were spent building a foundation — structured programming, calculus, and the habit of debugging things at 2 a.m. — and this semester is where the coursework starts getting genuinely interesting: data structures, digital logic, and object-oriented design.

Outside of class, I like translating what I learn into small working projects rather than leaving it in lecture notes. I'm particularly drawn to the layer where software meets hardware — logic circuits, low-level data structures, and the kind of code you can trace by hand. Long term, I'm aiming toward software engineering, with a growing interest in systems-level work.
